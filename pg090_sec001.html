Stage two: Posting to the general ledger

The general ledger posting involves the periodic (weekly or monthly) totals of the journal. These are posted to the sales returns account and the trade receivables account as they represent decreases in revenues (sales, through returns) and assets, respectively.

The entries made are therefore:

Debit: sales returns account

Credit: trade receivables account
