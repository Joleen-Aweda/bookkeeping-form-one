Debit note

It is a document prepared and sent by the seller to the buyer to adjust for an undercharge on the invoice because of various reasons.

For example, if the buyer has failed to return some of the containers (bottles) or packing materials that were not charged for in the invoice, the seller has to send a debit note to claim the undercharged amount.
