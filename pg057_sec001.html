Credit note

It is a document sent by the seller to the buyer to correct an overcharge on an invoice.

For example, if some of the goods supplied to the buyer are returned to the supplier for various reasons, for example, supplier delivering wrong or damaged goods, then a credit note will be issued by the seller.
