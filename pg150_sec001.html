A statement of financial position

Unlike the income statement that reports a financial performance (profit or loss) for a period, a statement of financial position shows the financial position of an enterprise at a specific date.

The specific date is the end of the accounting period.

The statement of a financial position is therefore prepared by enterprises to present its financial position as at the date indicated on that statement.

The statement of financial position of the enterprise shows the relationship between assets, liabilities and capital.

You will recall that, the basic accounting equation states that assets are equal to liabilities plus capital.

The statement of financial position, therefore, aims at showing how this equation stands at the end of the accounting period, considering in each of these elements.

The statement of financial position is referred to as such because it shows the assets of the enterprise and how such assets have been financed that is proportions of liabilities and capital.

Preparing a statement of financial position

When preparing a statement of financial position, the value of each category of assets and liabilities is usually taken from the general ledger balances.

To make it easy for the users to understand well the financial position of the business, there is an orderly system used in presenting assets and liabilities, rather than listing them randomly.

The common methods used in presenting assets and liabilities are explained in the following sections.
