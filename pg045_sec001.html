Steps to be followed when identifying and posting transactions in the ledger accounts:

Step 1:In each transaction, a student or book-keeper must identify two accounts involved in the transaction.

Step 2:Find out the element to which each of the accounts involved belong that whether it belongs to assets, liabilities, capital, revenue or expenses.

Step 3:Indicate the effect of transaction that is, increase or decrease of the account

Step 4:Debit and credit appropriate ledger accounts by applying the rules of double entry.
