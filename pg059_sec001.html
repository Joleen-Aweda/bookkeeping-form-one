Statement of account

This is the document prepared and sent by the seller to the buyer at the end of every period (usually each month) acting as a reminder to the buyer to pay the outstanding balance.

The statement of account contains information concerning the details on the invoices that were issued to the customer during the period, the amount paid by the customer during the period and the outstanding balance at the end of the period.
