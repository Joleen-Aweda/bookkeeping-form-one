Sales daybook or sales journals

When the supplier sells goods on credit, he or she prepares and sends an invoice to the buyer.

The invoice is usually prepared in duplicate, and the seller remains with a copy after issuing the original invoice to the buyer.

The retained copy serves as a source document that helps the seller to prepare the sales daybook.

Therefore, the sales daybook is used to record transactions related to credit sales of goods.
